### Get to Know Us Hi there! We're Hampie Hugs — a little gifting brand started by three sisters who share a love for thoughtful gestures, meaningful surprises and the joy of making someone feel special. What began as a simple idea between us has grown into a space where we bring creativity, care and a little bit of ourselves into every gift.

It started, as many of our conversations did, with the thought of doing something of our own.

Whenever the three of us got together, we'd talk about starting something, building something, and having something that was ours. We just never quite figured out what that something would be.

But there was one thing we had always been doing without even realising it.

In our family, we were the ones planning the little celebrations, arranging the surprises, picking up the cakes, setting the mood and making sure every gift was wrapped just right. We loved being part of those little moments that made people feel special.

So when gifting hampers started becoming a thing, the people closest to us were quick to notice the connection. They kept telling us, "Why don't you girls do this? You're already good at it!"

And one day, we finally did.

We started with an Instagram page, a name, a logo and the excitement of turning an idea into something real. Our first orders came from the people who knew us best — our family and friends, our very first supporters, who believed in us and wanted to see us succeed.

Their encouragement meant more than we can put into words.

Slowly, more people began discovering Hampie Hugs. Enquiries turned into orders, and what started as a little idea between three sisters began taking shape as a brand of its own.

We're still growing, still learning and still finding our way. But one thing has remained the same: we started this because we wanted to do something we genuinely enjoyed.

And in that, we've already found our little success.

Hampie Hugs is our little idea turned into something real — and we're just getting started.
